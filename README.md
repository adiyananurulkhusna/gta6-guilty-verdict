A GTA 6 launch-day meme in the form of a Leonida County Court verdict: after thirteen years of waiting, the People of Leonida finally put Rockstar Games on trial. The charge is thirteen years of aggravated waiting. The evidence is six exhibits (one trailer shown in 2013, two delays and zero releases, 214 GB stuck at 99%, 1,337 trailer rewatches and zero games, one couch worn through, 4,811 days and counting). The verdict is GUILTY, and the sentence is to release GTA VI immediately — with the court costs paid by the gamer's AI agent on make.fun. The joke is the trial itself: the wait was not an accident, it was a crime, and the court finally said so.

The meme is posted on X from the public account `@Adiyamanmonad`, with the bounty page link, a tag of `@makefunai`, and the account's play code in the post's text. The post's first picture is the meme (square PNG); its address goes in `POST.json`. The ghost in the picture is make.fun's real mascot, taken from the press kit at https://make.fun/press/ (the white ghost with sparkle eyes and its magenta star coin), set inside the document's magenta court seal at the top right, so it is part of the document — not a sticker in a corner. It stays recognisable when the picture is shrunk to 480 by 480 pixels.
